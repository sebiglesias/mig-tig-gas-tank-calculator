Frequently Asked Questions

How long will an 80 CF shielding gas tank last?
At a standard flow rate of 20 CFH, an 80 CF cylinder provides exactly 4.0 hours of continuous arc-on trigger time. Because most fabricators have an arc-on duty cycle of 25% to 35% (the rest spent measuring, clamping, and grinding), an 80 CF tank will last roughly 12 to 16 shop hours.
Why does setting CFH too high cause porosity in welds?
Excessive flow rates (> 30–35 CFH on small nozzles) generate turbulent gas flow instead of a smooth laminar shield, which sucks atmospheric oxygen and nitrogen directly into the molten weld pool, causing porosity.
What is the best shielding gas for mild steel MIG welding?
A 75% Argon / 25% CO2 mix (commonly called C25) is the industry benchmark for short-circuit MIG on mild steel. It provides minimal spatter, stable arc transfer, and deep penetration.
